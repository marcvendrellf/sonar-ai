## Yuka

Yuka says users scan food and cosmetic labels to receive an at-a-glance evaluation, inspect factors behind the result, and find healthier recommendations. The page shows color-coded scores and specific reasons such as additives, sugar, calories, allergens, and irritants. Yuka also says brands and manufacturers cannot influence scores or recommendations.

## Shopify

Shopify presents a product and storefront workflow built around structured product information, product cards, search and filtering, recommendations, visual editing, and publishing a usable online store. The page emphasizes turning merchant content into a browsable store rather than leaving it in an administrative table.

## Google Lens

Google Lens lets users make an image or camera view the query. Its product page lists visual identification, finding similar products, copying serial numbers and other text, and translating visible text.

## CARFAX

CARFAX presents a vehicle-history report keyed to a specific vehicle. The report gathers dated records and flags from available sources for use in a purchase decision. The interaction model is identifier, consolidated history, evidence inspection, then decision support.

## Product-pattern limitation

These products provide interaction references, not evidence that the proposed Cala fusion is technically possible. Brand-to-company matching, barcode ownership mapping, and Cala response fields still require tests.
