## Event facts

Anthropic says it partnered with Cerebral Valley, selected 500 participants, supplied each with $500 in API credits, and gave them one week to build. Claude-team judges selected five highlighted winners and prizes from a $100,000 pool of Claude API credits. Four of the five winners were not professional developers.

## Winners and relevant product loops

- **CrossBeam, first place.** Builders drag blueprints and correction letters into the product. Parallel agents parse documents, build a spatial index, and address each correction. The output is an approval action plan. The tool also supports municipal reviewers. The source says a city was considering adoption.
- **Elisa, second place.** A block-based visual IDE lets children assemble goals, requirements, agents, rules, and deployments while AI writes code. A task graph and teaching layer sit behind the visual blocks.
- **PostVisit.ai, third place.** A cardiologist built a patient and clinician workflow around what happens after an appointment. It explains diagnoses, analyzes notes, finds clinical evidence, and preserves physician oversight.
- **TARA, Keep Thinking prize.** It turns dashcam road footage into an investment appraisal. Vision analyzes road condition and roadside activity, then the product generates intervention costs, NPV, cash flow, sensitivity analysis, an equity score, and a PDF. The source says the former process took weeks and the prototype took five hours.
- **Conductr, Creative Exploration prize.** A browser MIDI instrument listens to a live performance and generates drums, bass, melody, and harmony. A C engine compiled to WebAssembly generates notes every 15 milliseconds while model decisions reshape the arrangement without stopping the music.
