## Gemini Live Agent Challenge

The Google Cloud post is dated 2026-05-16. It reports 11,878 participants, 1,536 projects, and 151 countries. The challenge asked teams to move beyond a text box and build real-time agents that see, hear, speak, and create.

Relevant winners:

- **ORION, grand prize.** A voice-directed operating-room copilot supplies answers, live data, and visual assistance without requiring the surgeon to touch a device.
- **drone-copilot, Live Agent winner.** Users talk with a drone to navigate, run visual inspections, describe surroundings, and confirm actions.
- **Sankofa, Creative Storyteller winner.** A few family details become voice narration, watercolor images, ambient sound, and a live conversation about family history.
- **Moonwalk, UI Navigator winner.** A hands-free desktop assistant operates mouse and keyboard workflows through voice.
- **Wand, multimodal integration and UX winner.** Users point at a screen and speak to click, search, and navigate.
- **NagarDrishti, honorable mention.** Voice reports from drivers populate a road-hazard dashboard for city officials.
- **Call My Parts, honorable mention.** A spoken part request starts web research and supplier calls, then produces a ranked dashboard.
- **Relay, honorable mention.** A webcam-based lab partner watches circuit building, catches mistakes, and combines physical guidance with a 3D sandbox.

## Gemini API Developer Competition

Google says the competition ran from 2024-05-15 through 2024-08-12.

Relevant winners:

- **Jayu, best overall.** A personal assistant integrated with a user's device.
- **VITE VERE, most impactful.** Personalized daily-life support for people with cognitive disabilities.
- **Prospera, most useful.** Real-time coaching during sales conversations.
- **Outdraw AI, most creative.** A party game where people try to draw images humans understand but AI does not.
- **Gaze Link, best Android app.** Eye-based communication for people with ALS.
- **Everies, best ARCore use.** Scans surrounding objects and turns them into interactive characters.
- **ViddyScribe, best web app.** Generates audio descriptions to make videos accessible.
