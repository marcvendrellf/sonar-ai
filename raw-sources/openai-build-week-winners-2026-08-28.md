## Event facts

OpenAI says nearly 47,000 builders from 186 countries took part over eight days and submitted more than 8,000 projects. The page announces eight winners in four categories.

The page states that judges evaluated technical implementation, design and user experience, potential impact, and idea quality.

## Winners and relevant product loops

- **Second Voice, first in Apps for Your Life.** It combines partial speech, a personal phrasebook, and immediate context to suggest a few likely sentences. The user selects or edits a sentence before the system speaks it.
- **veTriage, first in Work and Productivity.** A veterinarian with no coding background built it to help receptionists collect patient history, recognize urgent warning signs, and route cases without making clinical decisions.
- **Pulse, second in Work and Productivity.** It listens during cardiac-arrest response, interprets messy speech, maintains shared clinical state, and asks the team to confirm unclear evidence. Deterministic code tracks the workflow.
- **Echo Canvas, first in Developer Tools.** Users sketch a room, place sound sources and listeners, change materials, and hear the acoustic result immediately. AI authors and explains within constrained schemas while deterministic systems handle geometry and audio.
- **Sentinel, second in Developer Tools.** It combines deterministic static analysis, constrained model review, and isolated behavior probes to detect MCP security problems. The model cannot invent probes, cite nonexistent code, or silently erase findings.
- **Mechanica, first in Education.** It turns fragmentary historical evidence into interactive reconstructions of ancient machines. Dimensions link back to evidence or labeled scholarly inference, and disputed interpretations remain visible.
- **Dấu, second in Education.** Learners record Vietnamese words and compare their pitch curve with a validated reference. Deterministic signal processing evaluates tone while AI coaches. The app asks for another attempt when evidence is unclear.

## Directly stated lessons

The winner profiles repeatedly describe small interaction choices, human confirmation, deterministic state or calculations, constrained model roles, source traceability, and explicit handling of uncertainty.
