The documented rebuild command is:

```bash
pnpm --filter web build:saloon-shell
```

It writes an ignored geometry intermediate, creates the consecutive `UVMap` and
`Lightmap` layers, packs one shared atlas, bakes direct and indirect diffuse
illumination, and exports `TEXCOORD_0` plus `TEXCOORD_1` in the final GLB. The
lightmap is a 2,048 x 2,048 RGB half-float OpenEXR with a 16 px bake margin.

Blender 5.2.1 LTS used Cycles on the Apple M4 Metal device with 512 samples,
four diffuse bounces, one 3,400 K overhead area light at `(-2.8, 10.0, 1.0)`, a
6 m light size, 800 W energy, and low warm World illumination at strength 0.22.

The GLB and EXR load from the application origin. The retained HDR is no longer
requested by the Saloon. Nothing requests Blender, GitHub, Poly Haven, a CDN, a
Spline scene, or a Drei preset at runtime.
